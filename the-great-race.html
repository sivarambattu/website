The Great Race

Push the button, Max!

The Great Race - A really fun movie with great cast, Natalie Wood stole the show with her quite a chirpy, vibrant character. Very charming Tony Curtis. Jack Lemmon does the best as over the top evil professor as well as a goofy prince. Have a soft spot for slapstick comedies, this is one of the masterpieces at that.

Some of the highlights:
- the failed attempts of Professor Fate coming up with crazy contraptions
- the fight at the restaurant
- the cake fight at the end of the movie
