Matt Levine a genius mind that writes about finance in the most entertaining way possible. For many it might be educational as well, but for me I probably grasp about 20% of what he writes in his newsletter called Money Stuff. I will try capture some out of context quotes from his topics that can be some outrageous prediction that eventually becomes true or some deep philosophical pondering or just a silly proposition, ....., but one thing for sure, they are always fun.
