of high quality discussions around all aspects of life.

In this specific episode [Nature vs Nurture](https://www.econtalk.org/nature-vs-nurture-with-paul-bloom/), he talks with psychologist Paul Bloom about the widely debated topic of 
Nature vs Nurture, how it applies across different domains, interesting observations around it.
