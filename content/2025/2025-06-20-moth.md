Norm Macdonald has crazy creative capability to take an ordinary joke(perhaps a dad joke very often) and make it
into miles long journey, he often infuses some odd old-timey language into the mix.
A joke that can be told in 30 seconds, he can it milk for a good 5 minute routine, he weaves the intricate world around it.
He is a magical (shortest) story teller !
